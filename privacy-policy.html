Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 19, 2026

Introduction

This privacy policy applies to the Expiry Alert Pro app (hereby referred to as "Application") created by Rahul Lagariya as a Freemium service. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use our Service. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (IP address)
  • The pages of our Application that you visit
  • The time and date of your visit
  • The time spent on those pages
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device

The Application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. We collect this information to improve our service and provide better user experience.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) Technology

The Application uses Google Gemini AI technology to deliver personalized content, product recognition, and intelligent recommendations. AI processing is performed in accordance with this privacy policy and Google's AI Principles.

When you use AI-powered features:

  • Product images and text may be processed by Google Gemini AI for recognition purposes
  • AI-generated suggestions are based on your usage patterns and product data
  • All AI processing follows strict data protection standards
  • You can opt-out of AI features in the app settings

Google Services Integration

The Application integrates with the following Google services:

  • Google ML Kit: For on-device barcode and text scanning capabilities
  • Google Gemini AI: For intelligent product lookup and recommendations
  • Firebase: For authentication, cloud storage, and real-time synchronization
  • Google Play Services: For app distribution and updates

These services may collect and process data according to their respective privacy policies. We recommend reviewing Google's Privacy Policy for detailed information.

Data Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable.

Security measures we implement:

  • End-to-end encryption for sensitive data
  • Secure HTTPS connections for all data transmission
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Compliance with industry-standard security practices

Google Play Billing

The Application uses Google Play Billing for premium feature purchases and subscriptions. When you make a purchase:

  • All payment processing is handled securely by Google Play
  • We do not store or have access to your payment card information
  • Purchase history is maintained by Google Play and accessible in your account
  • Refunds and billing disputes are managed through Google Play policies
  • Subscription management is available through your Google Play account settings

For billing-related questions, please refer to Google Play's support documentation or contact Google Play support directly.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data stored in the Application
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt-out of AI-powered features
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time
